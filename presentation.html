SLIDE 1 / 12

1. Problem Statement

  • Core Task: Accurately classify 6 human physical activities in real-time using tri-axial smartphone accelerometer and gyroscope signals.
  • High Dimensionality: Each observation consists of 561 engineered features extracted from 2.56-second time windows.
  • Redundancy & Multicollinearity: Sensor axes measure shared spatial forces, resulting in massive collinearity across feature channels.
  • Postural Ambiguity: Distinguishing between stationary states with identical gravity alignment (Sitting vs. Standing).
Project Goal: Construct an end-to-end production ML pipeline that delivers ≥ 95% accuracy, minimizes computational overhead through PCA / Feature Selection, and evaluates model generalization across unseen individuals.
SLIDE 2 / 12

2. Motivation & Real-World Impact

🏥 Digital Healthcare & Assisted Living

Continuous monitoring of elderly patients, automated emergency fall detection, and objective gait tracking for rehabilitation.

📱 Pervasive Smartphone Sensing

Utilizes ubiquitous consumer hardware (Samsung Galaxy S II) instead of costly, obtrusive medical motion-capture laboratories.

⚡ Edge Machine Learning Constraints

Wearable devices operate with strict battery, RAM, and CPU limits. Compressing 561 features via PCA reduces edge latency and prolongs device runtime.

SLIDE 3 / 12

3. Dataset Overview

  • Dataset: UCI Smartphone Human Activity Recognition (HAR) Dataset.
  • Subjects: 30 healthy volunteers (aged 19–48) wearing a waist-mounted smartphone recording linear acceleration and angular velocity at 50 Hz.
  • Data Scale:
    • Total Observations: 10,299 samples (Training: 7,352 | Test: 2,947).
    • Input Dimension: 561 continuous numeric features.
  • Target Classes (6 Balanced Postures):
    • Dynamic (Moving): WALKING, WALKING_UPSTAIRS, WALKING_DOWNSTAIRS.
    • Static (Stationary): SITTING, STANDING, LAYING.
  • Quality Assessment: Zero missing values, zero duplicate records, outlier rate < 1%.
SLIDE 4 / 12

4. Approaches: Preprocessing & Unsupervised Discovery

  • Volunteer-Wise Grouping (GroupKFold):

    Partitioning folds strictly by subject ID prevents biometric data leakage. A model evaluated on random splits can recognize individual volunteer habits rather than the activity itself.

  • Feature Standardization:

    Applied StandardScaler fit exclusively on the training partition and mapped to the test set.

  • Unsupervised Clustering (Without Ground Truth Labels):
    • KMeans++ (k=2): Silhouette score peaked at k=2. Spontaneously discovered the natural divide between Dynamic (walking) and Static (stationary) activities with 99.7% alignment.
    • KMeans++ (k=6): Adjusted Rand Index (ARI) of 0.420; revealed that Sitting and Standing overlap heavily without supervision.
    • DBSCAN: Run on 10 PCA dimensions; identified 5 dense clusters and isolated 371 transition noise points.
SLIDE 5 / 12

5. Approaches: Feature Selection & PCA

  • Collinearity Filtering: Dropped features with pairwise Pearson $|r| > 0.95$, shrinking the candidate pool from 561 to 253. Shortlisted top 30 via ANOVA F-statistic.
  • Sequential Feature Selection (SFS):
    • Executed 3-fold cross-validated Forward SFS and Backward SFS down to 15 features.
    • 11 features were independently chosen by both methods.
    • Backward SFS + SVM achieved 87.51% accuracy using only 15 features (97.3% feature reduction).
  • Dimensionality Reduction (PCA):**
    • Projected 561 correlated channels into orthogonal principal components.
    • 102 components preserve 95% of total cumulative variance (an 81.8% compression).
  • Supervised Classifiers Evaluated: Logistic Regression, SVM (RBF), Random Forest, KNN, Decision Tree, and Voting Ensembles.

SLIDE 8 / 12

8. Experimental Results: Ensembles & Leaderboard

  • Soft Voting Meta-Classifier (Overall Best):

    Combines probability estimates from Logistic Regression + SVM + Random Forest: 95.96% Accuracy, 0.9588 Macro F1.

  • Hard Voting Meta-Classifier:

    Majority class voting achieved 95.93% Accuracy. Disagreed with Soft Voting on only 39 out of 2,947 test rows (1.3%).

  • 5-Fold Volunteer Cross-Validation (Generalizability):
    • Logistic Regression: 91.89% ± 4.29%
    • SVM: 90.92% ± 4.40%
    • Confirms robust generalization without overfitting to volunteer gait patterns.
SLIDE 9 / 12

9. Challenges Faced & Diagnostic Insights

1. Severe Multicollinearity

23.3% of feature pairs had $|r| > 0.70$. Resolved through correlation thresholding and orthogonal PCA decomposition.

2. Biometric Data Leakage

Standard random shuffling leaked individual volunteer gait traits. Successfully prevented by applying GroupKFold on subject IDs.

3. Sitting vs. Standing Confusion

53.8% of all errors (71 of 132 mistakes) were confusions between Sitting and Standing. Both share identical stationary gravity acceleration vectors.

SLIDE 10 / 12

10. Major Learnings from the Project

  • Curse of Dimensionality in Clustering: High-dimensional distance metrics collapse in 561-D space. Projecting onto 10 PCA dimensions enabled DBSCAN to detect meaningful density boundaries and noise.
  • The Pareto Frontier of Compression: 102 PCA components provide 98.5% of full-feature accuracy with a 4× speedup, making it ideal for edge microcontrollers.
  • Complementary Ensemble Behavior: Combining a linear boundary (Logistic Regression), a maximum-margin kernel boundary (SVM), and non-linear partitions (Random Forest) effectively smoothed decision boundaries.
